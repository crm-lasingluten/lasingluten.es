1. Definition and function of cookies

A cookie is a small file created by a website to store information on your device, such as preferences when you visit a site. When you access a web page that uses cookies, it asks your browser to save one or more cookies on the device. Cookies often store website settings, such as preferred language or location; so when the user returns to that page, the browser sends back the cookies that belong to it, making it possible to provide a personalised service and information according to their needs.

Cookies may also store information that personally identifies users. However, this website does not use this type of cookie and will only have access to the personal information you provide through the contact forms or by email. You can change your browser's cookie settings to automatically approve or reject requests to store cookies, delete them when the browser is closed, and other additional options.

2. Types of cookies

Cookies can be classified into three groups according to the following criteria:

a) According to the entity that manages them

  • First-party cookies: sent to the user's device from the website they are visiting.
  • Third-party cookies: sent to the user's device from a domain not managed by the owner of the website visited, but by another entity that processes the data obtained through the cookies (for example, Google). Since these third parties implement them, blocking and uninstalling them is governed by their own terms and mechanisms.

b) According to how long they remain active on the device

  • Session cookies: collect and store data while the user accesses a web page and are deleted when the browsing session ends.
  • Persistent cookies: remain on the device for anything from a few minutes to several years, during which time the data can be accessed and processed by the party responsible for the cookie.

c) According to their purpose

  • Technical cookies: necessary for the website to work; for example, they make it possible to remember whether the user has already accepted or rejected cookies.
  • Preference cookies: allow the presentation of the website to be adapted to the user's preferences, such as language, device resolution or the browser used.
  • Analytics cookies: allow statistics to be compiled on the volume of visits, web traffic and use of the different elements of the site, in order to improve it.
  • Marketing and/or advertising cookies: used to build a user profile and learn their preferences in order to show personalised advertising or content.

How we obtain your data

THE CONTROLLER obtains the personal data it processes through the following channels:

  • Provided voluntarily by the data subject, through a web form, email, telephone or contract, in order to request any kind of information or to enter into and/or purchase services or products.

Purpose of processing

THE CONTROLLER will process your data in order to respond to requests made through this website. In addition, if you give your consent to receive commercial communications, promotions and information of interest to you, commercial and/or advertising information may be sent to you through the usual communication channels. You may withdraw that consent at any time through the means provided for that purpose.

We will also process your data for commercial, administrative, accounting and tax management, in accordance with current legislation and depending on the information provided and the authorisation granted.

Data retention

The personal data provided will be kept for as long as the relationship with the company continues and the data subject does not request erasure, and in any case for the statutory periods that apply according to the purpose for which the data were collected.

If the data subject has voluntarily subscribed to any kind of commercial communications, newsletter, advertising, etc., the data will be kept until they request to unsubscribe from those communications.

Legal basis

The processing of personal data by THE CONTROLLER is based on:

  • The data subject's request for any kind of information.
  • The data subject's express consent to receive commercial communications.
  • The establishment of a commercial relationship arising from the purchase of products and/or services.
  • The performance of a contract.

Recipients

For as long as the data subject's data are being processed, THE CONTROLLER will not disclose the data to third parties, except where legally required.

Where third-party services are used for administrative, tax, accounting and/or commercial processing, the required data processor agreement will be duly entered into in order to guarantee the security and confidentiality of the data subject's data.

Rights

In relation to your personal data, you may freely and free of charge exercise the following rights:

  • Right of access to your personal data.
  • Right to rectification of your personal data when it is incomplete or inaccurate.
  • Right to erasure of your personal data when it is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.
  • Right to restriction of processing, of all or part of the data.
  • Right to object to processing.
  • Right to data portability.
  • Right to withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise them, you may contact THE CONTROLLER by email at info@lasingluten.es or by post at the address indicated.

If you believe your rights regarding the protection of your personal data have been infringed, especially where you have not obtained satisfaction in exercising them, you may lodge a complaint with the competent data protection supervisory authority.
